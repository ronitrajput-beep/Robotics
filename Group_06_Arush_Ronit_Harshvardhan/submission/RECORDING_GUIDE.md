# Short team presentation guide

This is a suggested recording plan, not a claim that anyone already recorded or personally wrote a particular script. Each member should explain code/results they actually understand. Use the completed final statistics/business graphs when ready; never read the preliminary calibration as a final experiment result.

- 0:00–0:30: All three members visible. State the PV-cleaning problem and show the off-panel home. Explain that this is a controlled MuJoCo study; no PV electrical recovery percentage is established.
- 0:30–1:30: Show dirt falling, real RGB feeds/boxes, persistent trigger, departure and the brush contacting dirt. Explain that dust mass transfers through a reduced-order capture/bin model; the intake/blower representation is not validated airflow. Show physical solid pushing into the permanent tray.
- 1:30–2:15: Show camera verification/revisit and return to the external home. Mention actual whole-panel footprint, all selected deposits cleared below threshold, fixed support/guide and guard-assisted containment. Give the actual completed final trial success counts at 10/20/30°; do not claim perfect field physics or arbitrary object collection.
- 2:15–3:15: Show the business sensitivity and queue/labour plots. State the main assumptions, measured simulation throughput, explicit manual bay-transfer labour and scenario payback/NCRI. Explain the break-even supervision fraction and why the model does not monetize unmeasured PV uplift.
- 3:15–3:45: Each member briefly states their actual contribution and one limitation/future test: camera generalisation, real-world localisation/traction, coating safety or hardware suction/charging validation. Include a source/provenance closing frame, not slides replacing the demonstration.

Use screen recording with all three webcams or a meeting recording. Rehearse until each person can explain the PI controller, RGB trigger, physical block interaction and business assumptions in their own words. Keep the final video between three and four minutes and enter its real LinkedIn URL in the Word control before signing/exporting.
